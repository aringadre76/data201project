# Airport and Route Performance Analysis

## Overview

This analysis examines performances of all the airports and different routes is present in the dataset.

## Research Questions

1. Analyse the performance of arrival of different airports?
2. Analyse the performance of different routes?
3. Rank all the airports by their overall performance, using on-time percentage and average delay?
4. Find the most unreliable route?

## Key Findings

- Airport arrival performance: ADK airport average 17.5 minutes early, and others notable airport like SPS and CDC. The worst airports are JST (83 minutes), HYS(67) and HGR(63)
- Route performance: based on small sample size, MCI -> PHX is the worst, at 1343 minutes delay, while DTW -> PHX is 51 minutes early.
- Airport performance rank: STL ranked number 1 base on average 11 minutes early and 100 on time percentage, while SAT is rank 33, with 15 minutes average delay and 40 percent on time.
- Worst performing route: route like ALB-ATL or SEA-PHX has 100% delay and 50% cancellation rate.
